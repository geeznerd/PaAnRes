A LITTLE RESCUE WITH A BIG HEART
Passion Animal Rescue started with one mission: reduce the stray population in Long Beach through spay & neuter, and help every animal we meet find a forever home.
We're 100% volunteer-run. No offices, no salaries — just neighbors, foster homes, and a lot of love. Every dollar donated goes straight to food, vaccines, and surgeries.
EVERY DOLLAR = DINNER, MEDS & BELLY RUBS
